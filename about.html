About us

Built for people who drive a long way to eat well.

Zaiqa started as a single stop on the National Highway (N-5), for travellers, families and truckers who wanted a proper hot meal without leaving the road. It grew branch by branch — Bahawalpur, Sadiqabad, Daharki, Mirpur Mathelo — each one keeping the same promise: fresh food, cooked to order, served fast enough for a highway break and warm enough for a family dinner.

What stays the same at every branch

Every branch grills its own seekh and tikka on-site, keeps a buffet line running through peak hours, and sets aside space for the weddings, birthdays and get-togethers that happen along the way. Bahawalpur and Sadiqabad each hold a dedicated banquet hall.

Qaswa Cafe

Alongside the Zaiqa branches, Qaswa Cafe in Sadiqabad offers a more relaxed, continental-leaning menu — pizza, pasta and shakes — for a different kind of evening out.
