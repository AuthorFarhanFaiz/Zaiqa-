Our story

Built for people who drive a long way to eat well.

Zaiqa started as a single stop on the National Highway, for travellers, families and truckers who wanted a proper hot meal without leaving the road. It grew branch by branch — Bahawalpur, Sadiqabad, Daharki, Mirpur Mathelo — each one keeping the same promise: fresh food, cooked to order, served fast enough for a highway break and warm enough for a family dinner.

Every branch grills its own seekh and tikka on-site, keeps a buffet line running through peak hours, and sets aside a hall for the weddings, birthdays and get-togethers that happen along the way.
