Frequently asked

Questions people ask us

How do I place an order online?

Go to the Menu section, tap "+ Add" on a dish (or "+ Add another item" for anything from the printed menu), then tap the cart icon and Checkout. Fill in your details and submit.

How do I pay?

Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa or Bank Transfer. For the last three, a 50% advance is required — our staff will call to confirm your total first, then you send the advance and share the reference.

Do you show prices online?

Not yet — our printed menus (viewable on the site) have the current prices. Staff will confirm your total by phone when you order.

Can I book a hall for a wedding or birthday?

Yes — Bahawalpur and Sadiqabad both have dedicated event halls. Message us on WhatsApp or call the branch directly to check availability.

Do you deliver?

Delivery availability and area depend on the branch — call or WhatsApp your nearest branch to confirm.

I created an account but didn't get an email — is that normal?

Possibly, while the restaurant is finishing email setup. Your account still works — just log in with the email and password you signed up with.

How do I contact a specific branch?

Every branch has its own page with address, phone number and a WhatsApp link — see the Branches section.
