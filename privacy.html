What we collect

When you create an account or place an order we store your name, phone number, email, delivery address and order details. Passwords are never stored in plain text — only a salted, hashed version.

How we use it

Solely to process and deliver your order, contact you about it, and — if you created an account — to show your past orders when you log in.

Payment information

We do not process or store card numbers. For JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank transfer, we only store the transaction reference you provide to confirm payment.

Who can see it

Only Zaiqa Restaurant staff, for the purpose of preparing and delivering your order.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your account and order history at any time by contacting your branch directly.
